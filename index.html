When someone responds

Your friend read what you sent. That's a big step. Here's how to walk with them from here, whatever they say.

"I prayed this"

Celebrate, and keep it personal. This is the best news you can get. Reply warmly, in your own words, and soon.

That's the best news I've heard all year. I'm so glad you told me. Can I ask what made you want to pray that?

Listen more than you talk. Ask what it meant to them. Don't rush to explain everything at once.

Offer one next step, not ten. Pick whichever fits them best:

  • Read the Gospel of John together, a chapter a day, and text each other one thing you noticed.
  • Come to church with you this Sunday. Offer to pick them up and sit together.
  • Meet a pastor at Keyport Bible Church to talk about what's next, including baptism.

Point them to Jesus, not to the words they prayed. Salvation rests on what Jesus did, not on saying a prayer perfectly. If they have doubts later, show them 1 John 5:13 and remind them God wants them to know for sure.

Let your pastor know, with your friend's permission, so the church can pray for and welcome them.

"I have a question"

Thank them for asking. Questions mean they're thinking seriously. That's a good thing.

I'm really glad you asked. That's a great question. Want to grab coffee this week and talk about it?

It's okay to say "I don't know." Try: "I'm not sure, but let's find out together." Honesty builds trust.

Talk in person if you can. Hard questions go better face to face than over text.

Bring in help. For big questions about suffering, other religions, or the Bible's reliability, a pastor is glad to meet with you both.

They're not interested, or upset

Thank them for reading. Don't argue, defend, or push.

Thanks for taking a look. I really appreciate it. I just wanted to share something that matters to me. Nothing changes between us.

Protect the friendship. Your love for them is often the most convincing thing they'll see.

Don't send more tracts unless they ask. Keep praying for them, and be ready if they bring it up later.

No reply

That's normal. Many people read and think quietly before they say anything.

Don't chase. After a week or so, one gentle check-in is fine:

No pressure at all, I was just curious if you had a chance to read what I sent and what you thought.

If there's still no reply, let it rest and keep praying. God's timing is better than ours.

They're going through something hard

Be present before you're helpful. Sometimes the best reply is "I'm so sorry. I'm here." Offer to call, visit, or bring a meal.

Involve your pastor for grief, crisis, or serious family situations. You don't have to carry it alone.

If they mention hurting themselves or being in danger, take it seriously and act now. Encourage them to call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. Then let a pastor know.

“Always be prepared to give a defense to everyone who asks you the reason for the hope that is in you. But respond with gentleness and respect.”1 Peter 3:15 · BSB

Remember: your job is to share faithfully and love well. Changing hearts is God's work. Pray for your friend by name, and be patient.

Questions about following up? Talk with a pastor at Keyport Bible Church.

Add Sent to your home screen

Sent will sit on your home screen like any other app and open full screen, ready whenever you want to share. There's nothing to download from an app store.

First, open Sent in your phone's browser. You're viewing it inside another app (like Facebook or Messages). Tap the menu in the corner and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome, then come back here.
This is a preview copy. It can't be added to a home screen. Open Sent from its web address on your phone, then tap Get the app there.

iPhones don't let websites add themselves, so it takes three quick taps in Safari:

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar. On newer iPhones, tap the More button next to the address bar first, then Share.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Leave Open as Web App turned on, then tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the menu in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Add or Install to confirm.

On a computer?

Sent is made for your phone. Open this page on your phone's browser, then follow the steps above. Or scan the QR code in the Send tab to open it there.

Can't find the option? Make sure you're in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), since some other browsers don't offer it.
